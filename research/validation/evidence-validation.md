# Evidence validation

Reviewed 2026-11-08. Independent, read-only research validation of the working checkout; only this report was written. Repository instructions read first: README.md, sources/evidence-classes.md, sources/README.md. Findings concern evidentiary support, not author intent.

## Summary counts

- **19 findings: 6 material, 10 minor, 3 notes.** Counts refer to the finding IDs below; repeated manifestations are not counted again.
- **Task A: 27 distinct URLs** actually have Accessed = `not recorded`/`unverified` in the SRC tables, rather than approximately 28. All were attempted and cross-checked against current principles. Verdicts: **16 SUPPORTED, 7 PARTIALLY, 1 NOT SUPPORTED, 3 UNREACHABLE**. Two additional user-named checks (Hamburger Menus and Response Times) give **17 / 8 / 1 / 3** across 29 table entries.
- **Task B: 15 patterns, three per directory, 45 selected Evidence bullets.** Exact-page support: **43 SUPPORTED, 1 PARTIALLY, 1 NOT SUPPORTED**. The two deficient citations are technically correct statements attached to insufficient/wrong pages; corrective sources were fetched.
- **Task C:** numeric scan covered all Markdown in principles/, patterns/, rubric/: **190 matching lines / 105 distinct spellings before normalization**, plus checks of unitless CWV values, counts, ranges without repeated units, and version numbers. Most values are traceable. The material unsupported general timing threshold is P-15’s sub-300 ms transition rule; additional authored test values and local citation gaps are distinguished below.
- **Task D:** no invented study was established. There are unsupported empirical/replication attributions and weak local citations. A separate Evidence section often supplies a real URL, so absence from the same section is reported without automatically calling the claim fabricated.

Material means a claim, classification, or gate can meaningfully change a review outcome without adequate support. Minor means narrower attribution, citation, or qualification trouble where the main recommendation remains supported. Notes record limits or explicitly authored conventions.

## Method and source access

A fetch was not counted as verification merely because it returned HTTP 200. I read substantive source passages, followed Apple’s official .md representations, and read Material’s official JSON text payloads when HTML supplied only a script shell. For web.dev I used `?hl=en` when content negotiation returned another language. I checked numeric graphics’ accessible text as well as body text (notably the LCP 4.0 s boundary). No browser interaction or paywalled ISO text was verified. Source-page summaries below are paraphrases; quoted bullets are repository text.

The exact register extraction was pipe-table column 4 (Accessed), not any occurrence of “not recorded” in a publication-date cell. Two registered paths are now stale: Norman is absent from current P-02; Material accessibility-basics was replaced with Designing in current P-06. They remain in Task A because they were explicitly in the requested register set.
